The **Roguelike** modifier adds a combination of critical elements from the [[Roguelike\|Roguelike]] subgenre to an existing subgenre. This may include procedurally generated terrain, randomized upgrades, and some lack of continuous progression (i.e., permadeath).

Due to its emphasis on mastering mechanics for improved runs, the Roguelike modifier has found prevalence in new entries of games in the [[10 - 19 Arcade Class/10. Arcade Class\|Arcade Class]].

### Examples

[[10 - 19 Arcade Class/12. Shooting/12.06. Top-Down Shooter\|Top-Down Shooter]]: *The Binding of Isaac* (Nicalis, 2011); *Star of Providence* (Team D-13, 2017)
- Note that is is also possible for the influence of *Crimsonland* (10tons Entertainment, 2003) to have led to the [[20 - 29 Adventure Class/23. Survival/23.00. Survival Genre#Horde Survival\|Horde Survival]] subgenre, with games like *Vampire Survivors* (poncle, 2022)

[[10 - 19 Arcade Class/12. Shooting/12.09. Rail Shooter\|Rail Shooter]]: *Rogue Flight* (Truant Pixel, 2024); *Whisker Squadron Survivor* (Flippfly, 2025)

[[10 - 19 Arcade Class/13. Brawling/13.02. Top-Down Brawler\|Top-Down Brawler]]: *Hades* (Supergiant Games, 2020)

[[10 - 19 Arcade Class/16. Platforming/16.03. Action Platformer\|Action Platformer]]: *Rogue Legacy* (Cellar Door Games, 2013); *Dead Cells* (Motion Twin, 2018)
